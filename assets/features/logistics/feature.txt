The rules are in WarfareRules (SupplyLimit, OverLimitAttrition, TerrainAttrition, WinterAttrition, NavalAttrition); defines: supply_limit_base, supply_limit_hub, supply_attrition, supply_attrition_max, winter_attrition, naval_attrition, fleet_away_days, river_attack_penalty, large_river_penalty, large_river_pixels."
