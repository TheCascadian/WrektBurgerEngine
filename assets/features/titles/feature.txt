A ruler who holds every county of a duchy may create it; claims on de jure land cost less. The ruler names a council of five (steward, marshal, chancellor, spymaster, chaplain: bonus 2% a point of skill) and one governor for each duchy (1% of tax a point of skill; -1 loyalty a year after five years)."
